# Scholaria 📚

## Overview

**Scholaria** is a centralized online platform designed to simplify the process of finding scholarships. It aggregates both local and national scholarship opportunities into one accessible system, helping students discover options that match their background and preferences.

## Purpose

Many students struggle to find scholarships that fit their qualifications and needs. Scholaria addresses this by organizing opportunities in one place and tailoring recommendations based on user-provided information.

## Features

- 🔍 **Centralized Listings** – Access a wide range of local and national scholarships in one platform
- 🧑‍🎓 **Student Profiles** – Users register and provide key details such as:
  - Location
  - School level (e.g., high school, undergraduate, graduate)
  - Personal preferences

- 🎯 **Personalized Matching** – Scholarships are filtered and suggested based on the student’s profile
- 📄 **Simplified Search** – Easily browse and compare scholarship opportunities

## How It Works

1. **Register** – Students create an account and input their details
2. **Set Preferences** – Indicate interests and eligibility factors
3. **Browse Scholarships** – View curated scholarship listings
4. **Apply** – Follow the provided information to apply for selected scholarships

## Target Users

- High school students
- College and university students
- Individuals seeking financial aid for education

## Goal

Scholaria aims to make scholarship discovery more efficient, accessible, and personalized—helping students focus more on their education and less on searching for financial support.

## Future Improvements

- Automated application tracking
- Deadline reminders and notifications
- Integration with scholarship providers
- Mobile application support
